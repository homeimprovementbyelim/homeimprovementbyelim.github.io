What we collect

  • When you ask for a quote: your name, email, phone number, address and postcode, details and photos of the job, and the consent choices you make on our form.
  • When you book a survey: your appointment time and the details you give in the booking form.
  • When you accept a quote or pay a deposit: the date and time you accepted, and your payment confirmation. Card details are handled by Stripe; we never see or store them.
  • If you are a business contact: your company name, your business email address, where we found it, and any replies you send us.

Who we share it with

We share only what each service needs to do its job:

  • Google: our email, documents and spreadsheets.
  • Cal.com: survey bookings.
  • Stripe: payments.
  • GitHub: hosts our website.

We use an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) to help write messages and quotes. We do not send your name, email, phone number or house number to it. It works only from a job reference, your postcode area (e.g. B91) and the job details.

We do not sell your data.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they use the safeguards UK law requires.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't go ahead: deleted 12 months after our last contact.
  • Customers: job and payment records are kept for 6 years (tax rules) and for the length of any guarantee we gave you.
  • Opt-outs: we keep your opt-out (just your email or phone number) permanently, so we never contact you again.

Your rights

  • object to how we use it, or restrict it;
  • withdraw your consent at any time.
