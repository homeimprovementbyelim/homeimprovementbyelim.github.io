Questions

How is the price worked out?

From our day rates, the size of the job and typical materials. Small jobs get a fixed price. Bigger jobs get a price range first, then a firm price in writing after we've seen the job. If we find something that couldn't be seen at the survey, we explain it and agree any extra cost with you before doing it.

Do I have to pay anything up front?

The survey is free. When you accept a firm quote we ask for a 25% deposit to book your dates, and the balance on completion.

Can I change my mind?

Yes. You have 14 days after accepting a quote to cancel, and we'll refund your deposit. If you ask us to start sooner, for example for an urgent leak, you'd only pay for work already done.

Who does gas and electrical work?

All gas and electrical work is carried out by contractors who hold the required registrations (e.g. Gas Safe, Part P) and insurance.

Which areas do you cover?

Coventry and within about 20 miles, including Nuneaton, Bedworth, Rugby, Kenilworth, Warwick, Leamington Spa, Solihull, Hinckley, Atherstone and Tamworth.
